# Seat Return prototype

A class prototype for an AI service design course. A season ticket holder who cannot attend a game releases the seat to the club in advance. The club resells it as an official resale ticket at face value three days before the game, and the holder gets part of the price back.

The club, teams, prices and seat numbers are fictional sample data. This is not a real service and is not affiliated with any club or ticketing company.

## Open it

- Both phones, linked: `index.html` or `?role=both`
- Buyer test: `?role=buyer&test=1`
- Season ticket holder test: `?role=holder&test=1`
- English: add `&lang=en`

## Edit it

Everything is in `index.html`. The service rules (deadline, opening day, payback rates, fee) are in the `RULES` block at the top of the script, and the screen text is in the `T` block in Korean and English.
